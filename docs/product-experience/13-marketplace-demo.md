## Marketplace Listing Copy

### Title
**WorkVouch — Verified Trust for Every Candidate**

### Short Description (160 chars)
Verify employment, collect vouches, and export trust scores to Greenhouse — automatically. Recruiters see verified trust data without leaving their ATS.

### Long Description

**Hire with confidence, not guesswork.**

WorkVouch integrates natively with Greenhouse to give recruiters instant access to verified employment history, peer vouches, and AI-powered candidate summaries — directly in the candidate profile.

**For recruiters:**
- Trust score and verification status in the Greenhouse sidebar
- AI candidate summary in seconds
- Employment timeline with verified dates
- Vouch consensus and "would rehire" signals
- One-click verification requests

**For employers:**
- One-click Greenhouse connection
- Automatic candidate linking by email
- Configurable automation (invite at Final Interview, export on score change)
- Integration health dashboard
- Trust score custom field export

**For candidates:**
- Mobile-first profile building in under 10 minutes
- Verified employment and peer vouches
- Privacy-controlled profile visibility

**Key features:**
- Native Greenhouse panel (no context switching)
- Real-time trust score sync to custom fields
- AI-powered candidate summaries and risk detection
- Automated invitation and reminder flows
- SOC2-aligned privacy (country/state location only)

### Categories
- Background Checks
- Candidate Experience
- Analytics & Reporting

### Pricing
- Free tier: Up to 10 candidates/month
- Pro: $99/month — unlimited candidates
- Enterprise: Custom pricing
